# Agnikund — Village of the Sacred Flame

A 3D Indian village survival game that runs in the browser. Keep the sacred fire burning, build your village by day and defend it from demons and ghosts of Bengali folklore by night. Survive fifteen nights.

**Play:** open `index.html`, or visit the GitHub Pages site for this repository.

## How to play

- Every job works from a building: build a Lakdi Taal for woodcutters, a Khadaan for miners, a Khet for farmers, then assign villagers.
- Press **Build** at the top to place buildings and **Remove** to tear them down.
- Each villager eats 4 grain at dusk. Build Jhopdis (huts) so travellers can settle.
- At night demons come out of the forest. Train Sainik and craft weapons at the Lohar and Aara Mill.

Controls: drag or WASD to move, mouse wheel to zoom, right-drag or Q / E to rotate, Space to pause, 1–3 for speed, B build, X remove, N messages, G graphics (Low / Mid / High). Works on phones in portrait and landscape.
