अग्निकुंड

Agnikund

Village of the Sacred Flame

Keep the fire alive. Build by day, defend by night, survive fifteen nights.

How to play

1

The Agnikund

Your village lives around a sacred fire that burns wood on its own. If demons put it out, the village is lost. Click the fire to upgrade it for more land, homes and buildings.

2

Day: build and work

Every job works from a building: a Lakdi Taal for woodcutters, a Khadaan for miners, a Khet for farmers. Build it, click it, then press Assign. Idle villagers pitch in as builders. Huts or fields built side by side join into bigger ones. A finished tower (Burj) claims a little circle of land round it, so you can build past the village edge.

3

Night: defend

At dusk everyone returns to the fire and demons come out of the forest. The warning says from which side. Sainik fight at the walls, Dhanurdhar shoot from behind them. Each fighter has a special move: the Sainik's shield bash stuns, the Gadadhari's slam shakes the ground, the Bhaladhari lunges through a line, the Dhanurdhar rains fire arrows. A fighter's gold Shakti bar fills as they fight; when it is full they unleash an ultimate. Big demons rear back before they strike and have power moves of their own. Later nights bring ghosts from Bengali folklore: when Nishi Dak calls someone, click them and hold them back; break a Mamdo Bhoot's grip before it twists a neck. Every few nights a great asura comes: Bakasura the grain-eater, Raktabija whose blood becomes demons (fire stops it), Kalanemi of the false copies, and Mahasura on the fifteenth. Watch the land: two days before, it gives an omen. Dawn burns them all away.

4

Grain and homes

Each villager eats 4 grain at dusk. Travellers settle at dawn if there is room, so build Jhopdis. At night villagers sleep in their own hut, or by the fire if they have none. When demons come near, villagers hide in their hut, or in the sacred grove if they have none.

5

Stockpiles

Workers carry goods in a tokri to the nearest stockpile with room. A Bhandar has 4 bays: click it, then tap a bay to pick the good it holds. Anything extra goes to the heap by the fire.

6

Upgrades and healing

Click a finished building and press Upgrade. Its own workers, your Mistri, idle villagers and any helpers you send rebuild it by day: more hands finish sooner. A trained Mistri works three times as fast as a helper, is the only one who repairs (at night too) and must lead all level 3 work. The wounded heal by the fire, at home, and slowly anywhere away from the fighting. A Vaidyashala's Vaidya heal them too, and its medicine kits turn a Sainik into a Chikitsak who heals soldiers in battle.

7

Materials: bricks, then iron

Level 2 is built with bricks: Beldar dig clay at a Mitti Khaan, and Kumhar fire 3 clay and 1 wood into 3 bricks at the Eent Bhatta, which can also burn wood into charcoal. Level 3 needs iron: set a Khadaan to dig the rusty iron-ore boulders, and Agariya smelt 2 ore and 2 charcoal into 1 iron at the Loha Bhatti. Bricks pay for the Agnikund, level-2 buildings and level-2 upgrades; iron for level 3 and beyond, weapons and tools.

8

Controls

Drag or WASD move · Wheel zoom · Right-drag or QE rotate · Space pause · 123 speed · H home · G graphics · while placing: R rotate, Shift place several.
