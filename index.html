अग्निकुंड

Agnikund

Village of the Sacred Flame

Keep the fire alive. Build by day, defend by night, survive fifteen nights.

How to play

1

The Agnikund

Your village lives around a sacred fire that burns wood on its own. If demons put it out, the village is lost. Click the fire to upgrade it for more land, homes and buildings.

2

Day: build and work

Every job works from a building: a Lakdi Taal for woodcutters, a Khadaan for miners, a Khet for farmers. Build it, click it, then press Assign. Huts or fields built side by side join into bigger ones.

3

Night: defend

At dusk everyone returns to the fire and demons come out of the forest. The warning says from which side. Sainik fight at the walls, Dhanurdhar shoot from behind them. Later nights bring ghosts from Bengali folklore, each with its own trick: click one to read about it. Dawn burns them all away.

4

Grain and homes

Each villager eats 4 grain at dusk. Travellers settle at dawn if there is room, so build Jhopdis. When demons come near, villagers hide in their hut, or in the sacred grove if they have none.

5

Stockpiles

Workers carry goods in a tokri to the nearest stockpile with room. Each camp and Bhandar shows what it holds; anything extra goes to the heap by the fire.

6

Controls

Drag or WASD move · Wheel zoom · Right-drag or QE rotate · Space pause · 123 speed · H home · G graphics · Shift place several.
